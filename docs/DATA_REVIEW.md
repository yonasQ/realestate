## Editorial decisions applied to all records

- **Prices:** none are published by Temer; all listings show "Price on request".
- **Amenities:** taken from each listing's text. The WPResidence theme's checkbox features (e.g. "Fireplace", "Central Air", "WiFi" ticked on every listing) were ignored as template defaults.
- **Images:** Temer's cover images are marketing flyers with Amharic text overlays and were excluded. Suspected stock photographs (generic kitchens and bedrooms reused across projects) were excluded. Interior renders shared across several projects are captioned as representative.
- **Construction status** is shown with the developer's wording and date (e.g. "Excavation finished (developer listing, May 2026)"), and delivery periods are labelled as developer estimates.
- **Map pins** are labelled approximate. Pins that Temer reused from a different area were removed.
